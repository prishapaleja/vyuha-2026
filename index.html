ABOUT

TRINITY

Trinity embodies the spirit of D.J. Sanghvi as its most anticipated annual socio-cultural, sports and technical festival.

A vibrant confluence of innovation, technology, culture, sports and social responsibility, Trinity brings together students to showcase their talents, challenge themselves and celebrate creativity.

With an exciting array of technical competitions, cultural events, sporting challenges and engaging initiatives, Trinity provides a platform for learning, collaboration and healthy competition.

Drawing participants from colleges across Mumbai, India and beyond, Trinity transforms the campus into a hub of energy, talent and diversity.

More than a festival, Trinity is a celebration of excellence, innovation, unity and the spirit of D.J. Sanghvi.

ABOUT

VYŪHA

Vyūha is the official inter-college sports event of D. J. Sanghvi College of Engineering, proudly organized by DJSCE Trinity.

The name, drawn from Sanskrit, means formation and strategy values that perfectly capture the spirit of this competition. Just as a Vyūha represents unity and collective strength, this event brings together diverse sports basketball, football, cricket, pickleball, and badminton each demanding skill, teamwork, and tactical brilliance.

By choosing Vyūha, we celebrate not just the games themselves, but the philosophy behind them: passion, discipline, and the power of working together. It is more than a title it is the identity of our grand sporting stage.
